[ Projects ]

The Projects That Bring the Strategy to Life

Educator Coaching, Mentoring and Development

Empowering teachers sits at the core of my leadership philosophy. I have developed a structured, continuous program of professional learning, coaching and mentoring that builds confidence, creativity and capability in digital pedagogy. Through a bespoke whole-school CPD calendar and a daily co-teaching mentorship model, I have supported staff in integrating AI, enhancing digital creativity and using Apple technologies to transform teaching and learning. Each session is intentionally designed to be part of a sustained learning cycle, encouraging experimentation, reflection and collaboration so that professional growth becomes an embedded culture rather than a single event.

Beyond the above, I have built capacity through the creation of our Innovation Drive Team, a community of staff innovators who lead digital initiatives across different year groups. This distributed leadership model allows digital growth to scale authentically from within, ensuring every staff member contributes to and benefits from the school's collective innovation journey.

The Innovation Hub

To make professional learning accessible to our entire school community, I designed and launched the Innovation Hub. The Innovation Hub is a central digital platform that serves as the heartbeat of Yasmina's technology ecosystem. The Hub hosts each of my on-demand training videos, weekly technology announcements and a curated library of AI integration guides, templates and walkthroughs created specifically for our staff. It enables teachers to engage in self-paced professional learning, revisit previous CPD sessions and stay updated with emerging tools and innovation updates across the school.

Beyond being a resource bank, the Innovation Hub represents a shift in culture. It empowers staff to take ownership of their professional growth, fosters collaboration between departments and ensures that innovation remains visible, practical, and sustainable. With over 80% of teachers accessing the Hub each term, it has become the foundation for continuous improvement and a model for how schools can embed professional learning into their daily practice.

Curriculum Design

I have designed a comprehensive Computing and Digital Learning curriculum roadmap that spans Nursery to Year 8, ensuring a coherent and progressive development of skills that transition smoothly into middle school. The roadmap is built around YBA's Digital Competence Framework, which leverages ISTE Standards for Students. Each year group has clear outcomes, practical applications and project-based learning experiences that foster both technical ability and creativity, building from play-based technology exploration in early years to problem-solving, coding and robotics in later stages.

The curriculum is enhanced through the integration of state-of-the-art equipment including LEGO Spike, mBot2 robotics, drones, 3D printers, BBC Micro:bits, alongside Apple technology such as iPads, Apple Classroom and the "Everyone Can Create" series. Its success led Aldar Education to request that it be shared as a model of best practice across other schools within the network.

Digital Competence Framework

In alignment with the key ISTE Student Standards, I developed a bespoke Digital Competence Framework. This framework ensures that students not only thrive in the digital world but also engage with technology in meaningful and effective ways, across other subject areas.

It is directly integrated into computing lessons and visibly displayed in classrooms, providing a clear roadmap for students to develop essential digital skills and literacy as they navigate an increasingly complex technological landscape.

Student Leadership

Each year, I coordinate the annual appointment of Digital Leaders in YBA, offering pupils the opportunity to act as ambassadors for technology and innovation. These students support peers, assist teachers and drive schoolwide digital campaigns, and enrol in various AI programs to build their confidence and competence with AI. I also guide and prepare students for external digital competitions such as Formula Ethara, Mubarmij50, FIRST LEGO League and the High Performance Learning Future Innovators challenge.

In academic year 24/25, our digital leaders won the HPL Future Innovators competition. Students created an AI-powered application called "Mathify", which visualises complex mathematical concepts for students. For winning the competition, the team worked with a professional app developer to create an MVP of their product.

Apple Genius Program

To further enhance student capability, I designed the YBA Apple Genius Program, a self-paced certification pathway that enables students to become mastery-level users of Apple technology. This program leverages Apple's "Everyone Can Create" framework, allowing learners to explore creativity through photography, video, drawing and music while advancing their technical proficiency in Keynote, iMovie and Freeform, with digital badges awarded at key milestones.

It has quickly become one of the most engaging enrichment programs in the school. This program runs in alignment with YBA's Apple Teacher Program, an Apple-certified program all staff are encouraged to complete during induction week.
